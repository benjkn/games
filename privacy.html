Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This policy describes how Tabletop (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website at https://games-101.playfree.party/ (the “Site”). Tabletop is a free collection of card and board games that you can play in your browser.

Information we collect

We collect the following kinds of information:

  • Usage and device data. Pages and games you open, approximate duration, game outcomes, and similar events. We also store a random player identifier in your browser so we can tell return visits apart without knowing who you are.
  • Account data (optional). If you create an account, we store your email address, a hashed password, and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and, when Google provides them, your email address and name.
  • Multiplayer data. If you play online, we process your display name, room/session identifiers, and game moves so the match can run. Chat messages in a table are delivered to the other player in that session.
  • Feedback. If you submit a bug report, we store the message and any name you include.
  • Local game state. Your browser may keep sound preferences and a short snapshot of the current game so you can continue after a refresh.

How we use information

We use this information to operate and improve the games, understand which games people play, and respond to feedback. We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and in-game events (for example, starting or finishing a game). Google may process IP address, device and browser information, and cookies or similar identifiers on our behalf. See Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

The Site uses browser cookies and local storage for analytics, a random player ID (for feedback context), sound preferences, and saved game progress on your device. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; some features (analytics, remembering progress) may not work if you do.

How we share information

We share information with:

  • Google — Analytics and fonts.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the Site and its users.

Children

The Site is a general-audience games collection and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention

Analytics data are retained according to Google’s settings for our accounts. You can delete local storage in your browser at any time.

Your choices

You may decline optional cookies in your browser or stop using the Site. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Contact us to make a request.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a privacy request: open an issue at github.com/benjkn/games_101/issues.
